[2026] deep cuts

I think taste can be acquired. You're not born with a taste in music: you listen, you discover records, and slowly you acquire a certain taste.

I built this because I was not into Spotify/Apple Music's recommendations, they seemed unpredictable. With deep cuts, I get records that share something with the ones I love: same producer, a sample, a label. Every pick comes with a reason, so I actually learn something with each dig.

deep cuts helps you discover new artists and records through what people have documented online about them: producers, session players, samples, labels.

https://digdeepcuts.com
Open source on GitHub

About

I made my first website at 11 and never really stopped.

I love the browser. I love the web, and the software we use every day to shop, to learn, to talk to people, to check the weather and get directions.

Today I design that software for luxury houses: Global Digital Experience Director at Givenchy, before that Acne Studios in Stockholm, and Net-A-Porter and Apple in London.

I believe that today, technology shapes our culture.
